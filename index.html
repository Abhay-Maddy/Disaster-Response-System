Core Capabilities

Everything You Need in a Crisis

🚨

Emergency Alerts

Receive real-time AI-generated alerts for earthquakes, floods, cyclones and other threats — directly to your dashboard.

🆘

One-Tap SOS

Press the SOS button to instantly dispatch your GPS location to the nearest emergency responders and your family contacts.

📡

Live Disaster Map

Interactive real-time map showing active incidents, AI prediction zones, rescue teams, and your current location.

🚑

Rescue Tracking

Track the status of your emergency request from submission to responder dispatch to resolution in real time.

🏠

Safe Shelter Finder

Discover verified government-approved safe zones and emergency shelters nearest to your location during a disaster.

🤝

Volunteer Network

Connect with trained volunteers and community responders in your area who can provide immediate on-ground assistance.

AI Threat Prediction

Neural network models analyze weather patterns, seismic data, and historical records to predict disasters before they happen.

Community Reporting

Report local incidents with photos, GPS coordinates, and severity levels. Verified reports are dispatched to authorities instantly.

Help in 3 Simple Steps

Register / Sign In

2

Report or Receive Alerts
